Games

The Fables games are short daily puzzles, each a small gym for a skill that AI tools now do quickly, so we keep the judgment to check their work. DataFables, CodeFables and ScaleFables are live, and more are on the way.

Why Games? The Cognitive Gym

The Industrial Revolution brought machines stronger than any person, and many people lost both their work and the identity built on it. We didn't go back. We kept the need for strength and dexterity, and moved it into sports, gyms, and hobbies, things we do because they're enjoyable and good for us.

AI is the same kind of shift for the mind. It now outperforms us at knowing things, not just at the mechanics of writing code, cleaning data, and drafting text. Knowledge we no longer need to hold in our heads will fade unless we choose to exercise it. One answer is a cognitive gym: games that keep our knowledge sharp and build the critical thinking we need to oversee and check what AI produces.

That's what the Fables games are. And there's a nice irony: the same AI that holds the knowledge behind these puzzles is what lets us build them, without anyone having to supply it all by hand.

Every Fables game works the same way. Each day brings one new puzzle, climbing from the easiest on Monday to the hardest on Saturday, with a Sunday capstone that mixes the week's ideas. When you want more, Unlimited mode serves extra practice puzzles that never touch your daily streak, and when you finish you can share a spoiler-free grid of your result, while your streaks and stats stay on your own device. Each game brings its own subject and puzzles, and the rest comes from a shared foundation, FablesCore, so an improvement there reaches every game.

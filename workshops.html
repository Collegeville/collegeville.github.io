Collegeville Workshops on Scientific Software

From 2019 through 2021, this series brought together community leaders and practitioners in scientific software from industry, research labs and academia. Each workshop took on one theme, and its white papers, presentations and recordings remain available on its own site.

The workshops

Software sustainability

St. John's University, Collegeville, Minnesota

Characterized scientific software sustainability, described impediments to improving it, and identified possible approaches.

Why these workshops

Corporations, research labs and universities all develop, use and support scientific software. Each tends to have its own business model and priorities, yet their products depend on one another. We believe bringing these communities together is what set this series apart. The goal was to identify the challenges they share and the differences that matter. Each workshop was planned by an organizing committee from across these communities, and its site lists the members.

How the series evolved

We originally expected to alternate between software sustainability and developer productivity. We then moved to choosing an emerging theme each year, which led to software teams in 2021. The first workshop met on campus at St. John's University. The next two were virtual because of the pandemic, and each spent its three days moving from challenges to technical approaches and then cultural approaches for improvement.

After 2021

The 2022 workshop was postponed to 2023 because of continued complications from the pandemic. The 2023 workshop was then not held because of continued logistics and staffing challenges. The series has concluded.
